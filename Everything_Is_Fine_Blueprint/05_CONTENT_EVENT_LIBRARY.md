## An MVP mission with varied paths: Big Randy's BBQ
**Setup:** Randy needs charcoal. The player picks up bag from garage and carries it to grill.

**Candidate catalyst A:** Dog steals burgers when the player leaves food exposed. Player may chase dog or use dog toy; dog drops burgers somewhere plausible. If near sprinkler switch, water activates.

**Candidate catalyst B:** Earl's automated grill flipper appears if player has discovered the garage. Player can disable it, move the platter, or let it fling food onto a nearby surface.

**Candidate catalyst C:** Granny offers to deliver charcoal by golf cart. If accepted, cart takes a short unpredictable route through yard; player can stop/redirect or work around her arrival.

**Payoff requirements:** At least two materially different terminal states; distinct funny lines; results accurately reflect triggered events. The BBQ can succeed imperfectly. A “failed” BBQ earns a comedic recap and continuation, never a hard stop.

## Ambient micro-gags (1–5 seconds each)
- Neighbor dramatically measures grass with tiny ruler.
- Dog walks by carrying a huge garden gnome.
- Earl tests a self-opening mailbox; it repeatedly shuts in his face.
- Linda stops to re-align a crooked street sign while a tiny disaster unfolds behind her.
- Granny honks twice at a stationary squirrel.
- Randy practices announcing his BBQ to an empty picnic table.

## Content authoring guideline
Write every beat as a *cause → visible reaction → playable choice → consequence*, not just a funny line. Use 2–4 short dialogue options per event based on state, all captioned. Keep the actual generated run sufficiently predictable for QA while varied for players.
