# Design Decisions, Open Questions, and Future Expansion

## Confirmed creative decisions
- Game title working choice: **Everything Is Fine!**
- Neighborhood setting: **Pleasant Pines**.
- Humor and fun are primary, not a side feature.
- Player is semi-normal; NPCs and world are source of comedy.
- Missions plus incidental free-roam events.
- Procedural generation must produce **comedy**, not only scenery.
- Sandbox-targeted; Claude Code / Builder Hub development workflow.

## Practical defaults, not yet explicit creator approvals
- Stylized low-poly 3D; family-friendly tone.
- Single-player MVP, one detailed street, four core residents.
- Short 3–7-minute favors, not a sprawling quest campaign.
- Lightweight persistent memory and end-of-round news summaries.
- On-device/runtime rule-based director, no paid AI APIs needed.

## Must verify before locking architecture
- Which Sandbox editor, SDK, API, and deployment environment is actually used by this repository.
- Supported third-party engines/assets/scripts and hosting.
- Physics/AI/navigation, online persistence and networking limits.
- Target input devices and minimum performance specs.
- Whether title/logo is sufficiently distinct and rights-clear.

## Future expansions (not MVP)
- County fair weekend, neighborhood weddings, yard-sales, block parties.
- Neighborhood daily newspaper and recap archives.
- More neighbors with intersecting rivalries and shared grudges.
- Player cosmetic customization.
- Friendly multiplayer, co-op interventions, spectator camera (only after feasibility).
- Community shared seeds/challenges, replay share codes.
- More sophisticated physical interactions and home interiors.
- NPC relationship arcs and seasonal neighborhood events.

## Three guiding questions for every new feature
1. Does this create a laugh or make a funny story more understandable?
2. Can it combine with existing systems in at least two distinct ways?
3. Can a player interact with or influence it rather than merely watch it?
If not, reconsider its priority.
