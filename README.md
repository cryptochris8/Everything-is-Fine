# EVERYTHING IS FINE!
*Welcome to Pleasant Pines.* Good neighbors. Bad decisions. Endless possibilities.

A procedural comedy game for **The Sandbox Studio** (Genesys engine 18) by AppalachAI Studios. You're the one relatively normal person on a street of lovable lunatics. Help Big Randy with his cookout, and the Procedural Comedy Director turns your errand into a chain of connected disasters:
- a dog heist;
- a burger-flipping robot;
- a sprinkler timer;
- Granny's golf-cart "shortcut".

Every day plays differently, the neighbors remember, and Channel 4 reports what *actually* happened.

- **Play locally:** `bash tools/check.sh --serve`, then go to http://127.0.0.1:5207/.
- **Test:** `./node_modules/.bin/tsx --test tests/*.test.ts`
- **Status, docs and architecture:** see `PROJECT_STATUS.md`.
