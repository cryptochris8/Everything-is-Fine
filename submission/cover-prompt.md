Create a landscape video-game cover illustration, 1536×1024, for a cartoon comedy game called
"EVERYTHING IS FINE!".

STYLE: match the attached screenshots, the game's own art: chunky low-poly 3D cartoon characters with simple round
faces, big friendly eyes and thin dark outlines; a sunny suburban street; soft bright daylight; clear blue sky with
puffy clouds; pastel houses (blue-grey, cream, pink, mint green, peach with a red roof); bright green lawns, white
picket fences, low-poly pine trees and round trees. Cheerful, clean, colourful. Polished mobile-game key art in the
spirit of a TV sitcom opening, not photoreal.

THE JOKE: the hero is cheerfully pretending everything is fine while the whole neighbourhood falls apart behind them.

HERO (centre foreground, biggest figure): the new neighbour from the attached images: short brown hair, teal T-shirt,
blue jeans, white sneakers. A huge, slightly forced, too-wide smile, one hand up in a friendly wave, looking straight
at the viewer. Calm and sunny while chaos happens all around.

THE CHAOS BEHIND THEM (every character must match the attached cast sheet, same outfits and colours):
- BIG RANDY: big bearded man in a tall white chef's hat, white apron over an orange-sleeved shirt, dark jeans. He
  proudly flips burgers at a kettle grill that is shooting a tall column of flame.
- UNCLE EARL: bald inventor with round glasses in an olive-green jumpsuit, cranking a home-made wooden catapult that
  has just launched a big pink-and-white birthday cake high into the air, arcing toward the hero's roof.
- On the roof: a squad of small brown squirrels waiting for the cake with napkins tucked in.
- ADMIRAL BISCUIT: a round orange tabby cat wearing a little navy captain's hat with a gold button, riding a grey
  robot vacuum down the street like a ship's captain.
- LINDA: blonde bob, white sunglasses, lilac blouse, navy skirt, holding a clipboard, chasing a flurry of white HOA
  paperwork pages blowing through the air.
- A few white chickens in the yard happily "signing" those papers with pens.
- GRANNY DARLENE: white hair in a bun, round glasses, lilac cardigan, plum skirt, cheering with a raised fist.
- TODD: kid in a lime T-shirt, navy shorts and an orange headband, mid-flip, bouncing off a trampoline.
- BRISKET: a cheerful golden puppy running past with a garden gnome in his mouth.

TITLE: across the upper middle, a bright bubbly sitcom-style logo reading "EVERYTHING IS FINE!" in chunky rounded
letters: cream-white fill, a thick navy outline and a warm orange 3D drop shadow, with a slightly wobbly, bouncy
baseline. Directly under it, a small sky-blue ribbon banner reading "GOOD NEIGHBORS. BAD DECISIONS." Spell every word
exactly, including the exclamation mark. No other text anywhere.

COMPOSITION (important): keep the title, the hero's face and the main gags well inside the centre of the image. Leave
a clear margin of about 10% at the top and bottom edges and 7% on the left and right with nothing important in it,
just sky, lawn and street, because the image will be cropped on every side. The hero should be the clear focal
point; the gags frame them without hiding the face or the title.

DO NOT include: a dog sitting in a burning room or any "this is fine" meme imagery; fire inside a house; any real
brand names, logos or products; a watermark or signature; any text besides the title and the banner; realistic or
photographic rendering; scary or violent imagery.
